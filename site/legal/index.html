Legal notice and privacy

Who publishes this site, who hosts it, and what it knows about you: very little.

Last updated on 22 October 2026.

Publisher

art.steeve.website is published by Steeve Pommier, a private individual, on a non-commercial basis: it is free, carries no advertising and sells nothing. The sketches are his own work.

Contact: through the site's repository on GitHub.

As a non-professional publisher, the identification details required by French law (Loi pour la confiance dans l’économie numérique, art. 6-III) are held by the host below.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages: GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States, github.com. Visit counting runs on a server of the publisher's (see below).

Audience measurement

Visits are counted with Umami, self-hosted by the publisher at insight.steevepommier.com: no third party receives the data, and none of it is used for advertising.

What is sent, for each page viewed: its address (the path alone, without query string or fragment), its title, the site you came from, your screen size and your browser’s language. From the request, the server works out your browser, your operating system, the type of device and your country. No event is sent: what you do inside a sketch is never reported.

What never is: your IP address is not stored, and nothing can follow you from one site to another. No cookie is set.

Visits are only counted on art.steeve.website and costardrouge.github.io, and never when your browser sends Do Not Track or when you switch counting off here; that choice is kept in this browser.

Without JavaScript, this page cannot tell whether your visits are counted; the counter does not run without it either.

On your device

The site has no account and keeps nothing about you on a server. In your browser’s local storage, on this device only:

  • if you switch counting off above, the choice is kept under the key umami.disabled;
  • the control drawer of a sketch keeps its settings under the key sketch-ui-settings.

Some sketches react to sound, to a camera or to a MIDI instrument: your browser asks for your permission first, and what they read is processed in your browser — the site has no server to send it to. To erase what is kept, clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings.

Cookies and third parties

The site sets no cookie, so there is no consent banner: there is nothing to accept.

The gallery’s page loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google’s servers for it, as described in Google’s privacy policy. Like any host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the site: see GitHub’s privacy statement. The site itself has no server and keeps no log.

Your rights

The GDPR lets you ask for access to the data concerning you, or for its erasure. The site keeps none that names you: what is in your browser stays on your device, and the audience measurement carries no identifier. For any question or request, write through the site’s repository; you may also complain to the CNIL.
